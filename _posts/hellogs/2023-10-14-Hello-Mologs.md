# 为何存在 What is Mologs

Molog is the set of some blogs from my hand, which are mainly about somthing (I think) important, such as ideas(insights), knowledge summary and detailed studying notes.

*Thoughts and Insights* are the part I want to emphasize the most.

博客我个人感觉不应该是堆积如山的资料与知识，更多的是个人的想法，目的是为了能碰撞思想火花，所以在我的博客中不会有非常具体的知识或技术介绍，更多的是学习过程中的感想。
